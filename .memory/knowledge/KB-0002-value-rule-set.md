## Knowledge Captured

The organizational definition of value for the Hippo Digital Service Platform (HDSP) is driven by its vision to empower multidisciplinary public service delivery teams. Value is defined through four primary drivers:

1. **Delivery Velocity**: Improving the speed or ease with which teams can build and deploy services.
2. **Platform Reliability**: Enhancing the uptime, observability, or structural integrity of the platform.
3. **Accessibility & Compliance**: Helping meet WCAG 2.2 AA or other statutory requirements.
4. **Strategic Alignment**: Aligning with the core vision of standardizing public service delivery.

These drivers form the **Value Rule Set** used to assess new features and initiatives, ensuring they contribute to the platform's core mission and provide measurable benefits before proceeding to development.
